WHAT THIS DEMONSTRATES

Real transactions. Read-only checks.

This demo verifies completed transactions; it does not fund a new escrow or repeat signing. Public service responses are compared with the committed evidence. Signer decisions are recorded outcomes, labeled separately below.

The NEAR accounts and payment amount are public. The receipt carries no Zcash address, transaction ID, or amount. Observers may still correlate activity by timing.

TRUST BOUNDARY

The payer holds two shares.

This protects the payer and an honest payer’s automation. It does not protect a solver against a dishonest payer. The payer can bypass signer policy or withhold a fill claim.

Testnet only. Not audited. Trusted-dealer key generation, signer-enforced expiry, and no quantum recovery.
