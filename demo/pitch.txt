ironwood-intent-escrow: a 2-of-3 FROST escrow on Zcash's Ironwood shielded pool

Fills on NEAR. Refunds to a fresh shielded address.
Never writes a Zcash address, transaction id, or amount on NEAR.

Three shares, any two spend, none spends alone:
  payer.key      the payer                  signs the release after re-fetching the NEAR payment,
                                            and the refund
  solver.key     the solver                 signs only a release that pays the solver
  deadline.key   the payer's second device  signs only a refund, after expiry,
                                            with no paid fill claim

Two outcomes:
  fill     the solver pays wNEAR on NEAR, memo = the quote id
           -> the payer re-fetches and checks it -> payer + solver release the note
  refund   the quote expires unfilled
           -> payer + deadline device refund the note to a fresh address

Testnet only. Trusted-dealer key. The deadline device is the payer's.
